<h2>Facility Booking</h2>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Bookings can be made at the Sengkang CC Counter (Level 1)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Bookings can be made up to <strong><u>14 days</u></strong> in advance</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Please note that facility bookings are <strong><u>strictly</u></strong> for
leisure use only</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Kindly note that booking is subject to space availability as Sengkang
CC may use the spaces for events</p>
</li>
</ul>
