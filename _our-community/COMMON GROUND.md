<h1>COMMON GROUND</h1>
<p></p>
<p>Located adjacent to Sengkang CC's community hard court and facing Kopitiam
Square, this container serves dual functions as both a wellness lounge
and versatile event shelter, designed to attract more residents to actively
use and enjoy this community space.</p>
<p></p>
<p>As a wellness lounge, it provides a relaxing space for community members
to engage in light activities and discussions. The container includes a
small internal pantry for refreshments, with an external water cooler and
sink serving both lounge users and hard-court visitors.</p>
<p></p>
<p>During large-scale community events, the container transforms into a multi-purpose
shelter, functioning as a registration point, information booth, or small
group area. Its strategic positioning and adaptable layout enhance both
everyday community use and event-day operations, making it a valuable addition
to the open space.</p>
