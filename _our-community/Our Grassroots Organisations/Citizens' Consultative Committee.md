<p>The Sengkang North Citizens’ Consultative Committees (CCCs) is a vital
bridge between the people and the Government. The CCC plans and leads major
grassroots activities within the constituency, oversee local assistance
programmes, and organise major fund-raising projects and national campaigns.</p>
