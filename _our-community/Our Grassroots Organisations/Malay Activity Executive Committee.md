<p>The Sengkang North Malay Activity Executive Committee (MAECs) promotes
greater Malay participation in the management and activities of our various
grassroots organisations (GROs). The MAEC organises cultural, educational,
social, recreational and sporting activities to promote mutual respect
and harmonious relations between the Malays and other communities. Activities
organised by our MAEC include Silat classes, dance workshops, and Hari
Raya celebrations.</p>
