<p>The Sengkang North Community Sports Network (CSN) aims to foster community
bonding through a wide array of sports activities for residents to participate
and interact with one another.</p>
<p>Activities organised by the CSN include sports carnivals, sports clinics,
brisk walks, tournaments for bowling, badminton, pickleball and more. The
committee is made up of volunteers who assist to promote social cohesion,
racial harmony and community bonding amongst residents.</p>
