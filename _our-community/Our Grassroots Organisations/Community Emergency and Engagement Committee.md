<p>The Sengkang North Community Emergency and Engagement Committee (C2E)
strengthen community resilience through emergency preparedness planning,
training, and coordinated crisis response</p>
<p>They also work with community partners like SCDF to conduct house visits
to spread awareness of fire safety, host emergency preparedness events
across the constituency and organise events such as blood donation drives
where residents can join in.</p>
